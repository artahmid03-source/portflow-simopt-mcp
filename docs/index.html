  • Sources: AIS messages relayed by aisstream.io; water levels from PEGELONLINE, the open data service of the German Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration (WSV).
  • The tide forecast is a harmonic model fitted to the last 31 days of measurements, with the surge seen over the last tidal day carried forward. It is not an official prediction and must not be used for navigation.
  • Gauge zero at these gauges lies about 5.0 m below the German height reference NHN.
